🔹 Product FAQs

What are cleanroom wipes?
Cleanroom wipes (also called cleanroom wipers) are specialized cleaning cloths designed for controlled environments. They are manufactured with low particle generation, low chemical residue, and sealed edges to prevent fiber release. Unlike household wipes or paper towels, they undergo strict manufacturing processes to meet ISO cleanroom standards. They are used in semiconductor fabrication, PCB assembly, medical device manufacturing, pharmaceutical production, and other contamination-sensitive industries.
What is the difference between polyester and microfiber cleanroom wipes?
Polyester cleanroom wipes are made from continuous filament polyester fibers, knitted and laser-cut for low particle generation. They offer excellent chemical resistance and are ideal for ISO Class 5-8 environments. Microfiber wipes use split polyester and polyamide fibers for superior absorbency and particle trapping. Polyester wipes are preferred for general cleanroom cleaning and solvent application, while microfiber wipes excel where high absorbency and fine particle removal are needed. Yongxinyuan DS Series uses high-quality polyester+nylon knitted fabric.
What is the difference between laser cut, ultrasonic cut, and cold cut wipes?
Laser cutting uses a high-energy laser beam to seal and cut fabric simultaneously, creating the tightest edge seal with virtually zero particle release. It is standard for ISO Class 5-6 cleanrooms. Ultrasonic cutting uses high-frequency vibration to seal edges, suitable for ISO Class 7-8. Cold cutting is a mechanical cut without edge sealing for industrial-grade wipes where cost is prioritized. Yongxinyuan offers all three methods: laser cut as standard, ultrasonic cut as option, and cold cut for industrial grade.
What are ESD wipes and when are they needed?
ESD wipes are cleanroom wipes treated with anti-static properties to prevent static charge buildup during cleaning. They are essential in electronics manufacturing environments where static discharge can damage sensitive components. They are typically used in PCB assembly lines, electronics repair stations, and semiconductor handling areas where static control is critical.
What sizes of cleanroom wipes are commonly available?
Common sizes include 4x4 inches (10x10 cm), 6x6 inches (15x15 cm), 9x9 inches (22.5x22.5 cm), 12x12 inches (30x30 cm), and 18x18 inches (45x45 cm). Custom sizes are available. The choice depends on the application: smaller wipes for precision cleaning, larger wipes for surface wiping. Yongxinyuan DS Series is available in all standard sizes from DS-0404 to DS-1818.
What are industrial wipes vs cleanroom wipes?
Industrial wipes are designed for general manufacturing and maintenance cleaning where strict particle control is not required. They are more cost-effective and may use different materials and edge treatments. Cleanroom wipes are manufactured under controlled conditions with strict particle and residue limits, suitable for ISO classified environments. Yongxinyuan offers both DS Series for cleanroom grade and KH Series for industrial grade.

🔹 Technical FAQs

What is an ISO Class 5 / Class 100 cleanroom?
ISO Class 5 is a cleanroom classification permitting no more than 3,520 particles at 0.5 microns per cubic meter. These environments require strict gowning protocols, HEPA/ULPA filtration, and controlled airflow. Typical applications include semiconductor photolithography areas, aseptic pharmaceutical filling, and advanced medical device manufacturing. Yongxinyuan DS Series wipes are suitable for ISO Class 5-8 environments.
What are the ISO cleanroom classification levels?
ISO 14644-1 defines cleanroom classes from ISO Class 1 to Class 9. Key commercial grades: ISO Class 5 for semiconductor fabs, ISO Class 6 for cleanroom assembly, ISO Class 7 for pharmaceutical compounding, ISO Class 8 for general cleanroom manufacturing. Yongxinyuan DS Series wipes are suitable for ISO Class 5-8.
What does low particle generation mean for wipes?
Low particle generation refers to the wipe ability to release minimal particles during use. This is critical in cleanrooms where introduced particles can contaminate products. Testing involves mechanically agitating the wipe in a controlled environment and counting released particles. Laser-cut sealed edges significantly reduce particle shedding. Yongxinyuan DS Series wipes are tested for low particle generation to meet ISO Class 5-8 requirements.
What does low residue mean for cleanroom wipes?
Low residue means the wipe leaves minimal chemical residue after wiping a surface. This is important in semiconductor and medical device manufacturing where residues can cause defects or contamination. Testing involves extracting wipes with solvents and measuring dissolved solids. Yongxinyuan DS Series wipes undergo strict NVR control for low residue performance.
What are the key performance indicators for cleanroom wipes?
Key performance indicators include: particle count, non-volatile residue, absorbency rate, absorbency capacity, extractable ions, and fiber release. These metrics help buyers select the right wipe for their specific cleanroom class and application. Testing follows IEST-RP-CC004.3 standards.

🔹 Industry Application FAQs

What wipes are used in semiconductor cleanrooms?
Semiconductor fabs primarily use polyester cleanroom wipes with sealed edges, low particle generation, and low NVR for cleaning wafer handling equipment and maintenance procedures. ESD wipes are used in static-sensitive areas. Wipes must meet ISO Class 5-6 requirements with strict particle and ionic contamination limits. Disposable cleanroom wipes are standard.
What wipes are used in PCB manufacturing?
PCB manufacturing uses cleanroom wipes for SMT stencil cleaning, flux residue removal, and general surface preparation. ESD wipes are important for static control. IPA pre-saturated wipes are popular for quick cleaning of circuit boards. Wipes should be low particle and lint-free to avoid contamination on solder joints and component pads.
What are the cleaning requirements for medical device manufacturing?
Medical device cleanrooms typically require ISO Class 7-8 conditions. Wipes should be low particle, low residue, non-toxic, and suitable for cleaning surfaces that contact medical devices. Sterile or gamma-irradiated wipes may be required for certain applications. Documentation and traceability are important for regulatory compliance.
How are cleanroom wipes used in pharmaceutical production?
Pharmaceutical cleanrooms use wipes for cleaning production equipment, work surfaces, and facility areas. In GMP environments, wipes must not introduce contaminants or chemical residues that could affect drug products. USP 797 and EU GMP Annex 1 guidelines influence wipe selection for compounding pharmacies and sterile manufacturing.
