🏭 About Our Factory

Our Factory — Yongxinyuan (Longhua, Shenzhen Production Base) | 永新源科技（深圳龙华生产基地）

We own and operate our specialized cleanroom wipes factory in Longhua, Shenzhen — equipped with laser cutting, ultrasonic cutting, and cold cutting production lines. As a manufacturer-direct supplier, our DS Series wipes are produced in controlled environments suitable for Class 100~1000 cleanrooms, serving demanding industries such as semiconductor fabrication, PCB assembly, and medical device manufacturing.
